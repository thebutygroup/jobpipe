jobpipe

A job-discovery pipeline. It watches company job boards and searches continuously, scores every posting against what you said you want, and gives you a private page of ranked matches — with the reasoning shown, not just a number.

1 · Sign up

Fill in one form: the job titles you want, and — most importantly — a few sentences on what you're actually looking for. That free text is what drives the matching, so the more specific, the better the matches. A minimum salary is optional and only ever used to flag jobs above or below your expectations; it's never displayed.

→ Create your profile

2 · What to expect

Sign-ups are reviewed by a human before any matching runs — usually within a day. Your matches page address is simply your user name: /job_matches/<your-user-name>. Bookmark it; it's yours alone and shows no name, no contact details — just your matches. It stays empty until your first matching run completes.

3 · Reviewing your matches

Each match carries a score out of 10 from a strict screener reading the full job description against your profile. Open a match to see: ✓ green highlights where the job exceeds what you asked for, a "you want / the job offers" alignment table explaining the score, the full description, a relative salary signal, and links to the listing. Filter by title keyword and sort by score, title, company or recency. Apply directly with the company — the listing links take you there.

Site map: sign up · your matches (personal link from sign-up) · questions — ask whoever sent you here.
